---
title: "ADR-0005: Text document allow-list"
description: Restrict uploads to text-oriented office formats.
---

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Chat-with-document product should not accept arbitrary binaries (images, executables, archives).

## Decision

Central allow-list in `lib/document-types.ts` by extension (and MIME heuristics): PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, TXT, Markdown, RTF, ODT/ODS. Max 50MB. Enforced client-side and on presign.

## Consequences

- Pros: safer surface, clearer product scope  
- Cons: rejects valid edge MIME quirks unless extension is correct
