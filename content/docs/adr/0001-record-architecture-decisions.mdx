---
title: "ADR-0001: Record architecture decisions"
description: Adopt ADRs for significant technical choices.
---

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Documind evolved quickly (auth, storage, multi-chat, Redis). Without recorded decisions, onboarding and future trade-offs become tribal knowledge.

## Decision

Maintain Architecture Decision Records under this docs site (`content/docs/adr`). Each major product or infrastructure choice gets a numbered ADR.

## Consequences

- Pros: clear history, easier reviews  
- Cons: small process overhead on large PRs
