---
title: "ADR-0003: Presigned uploads to RustFS"
description: Browser uploads directly to S3-compatible storage.
---

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Documents must be stored durably. Proxying large files through Next.js increases load and timeouts.

## Decision

Use **presigned PUT** URLs (AWS SDK + RustFS). Metadata and ownership stay in Postgres. Confirm with HEAD after upload.

## Consequences

- Pros: scalable uploads, simple app servers  
- Cons: requires CORS and correct endpoint/region configuration
